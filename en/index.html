Frequently Asked Questions

Is it worth learning Arduino from scratch?

Yes, Arduino is one of the easiest entry points into embedded systems. I offer free courses on my YouTube channel starting from zero, plus consulting services if you need help with a specific project.

What is the difference between full project programming and consulting?

Full programming means I build your project from scratch (code and schematic). Consulting means you already have a working or partially working project and need a review or a specific fix.

Do you work on university graduation projects?

Yes, graduation projects are one of the main things I work on — whether Arduino, IoT, robotics, or even presentation design and 3D models for the project.

How do I order a service from you?

You can order directly through Khamsat, Nafezly, or Kafiil using the service links on the site, or reach me directly via WhatsApp or the contact form at the bottom of the page.
